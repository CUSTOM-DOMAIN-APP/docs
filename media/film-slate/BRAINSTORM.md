# CustomDomain™ film slate: brainstorm and picks

Two films exist so far:
- **"The human middleware"** (50 s): a kinetic, problem-first brand film.
- **"The end user's path"** (1:44): a line-drawn explainer, told from the end user's side.

The brief for this slate is ideas that differ from both of them, and from each other, in concept and in nature. Nature here means the kind of film: the viewer it is made for, the job it does, the grammar it uses and the way it moves.

## Nine ideas

| # | Working title | Nature | For whom | Concept in one line | Look and motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Under the Button** | Persuasive reveal (build vs buy) | Founders, CTOs, heads of product | The "Connect domain" button is the tip of an iceberg. ## How they were scored

- **Distance from the existing films:** concept, nature and look.
- **Distance from each other:** the three picks should not share any of these.
- **Value to the business:** who acts on it, and what it moves them to do.
- **Truth:** every claim must stand on the docs.
- **Craft risk:** can it be built to a high finish with the tools here?

## The three picks, side by side

| | Under the Button | Three Calls | 2:13 AM |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job | Persuade: buy, don't build | Demonstrate: it really is this small | Make the cost felt |
| Viewer | Founder or CTO | Engineer | Support or ops lead |
| Grammar | Continuous descent, one long take | Screencast with code zooms | Screen-life, real time |
| Look | Paper and deep ink, layered strata, depth gauge | An editor, a terminal and a preview in brand faces | A laptop desktop in 16:9 and a phone in 9:16 |
| Voice | British male, slow and documentary | American male, brisk and friendly | American female, quiet, an inner monologue |
| Score | Low drone, felt piano, sonar pings | Minimal house at 118 bpm | A ticking pulse that resolves to calm |
| Length | about 60 s | about 45 s | about 60 s |

Each film gets the same workflow as the last one:
1. Concept.
2. Research.
3. Script, order of events, visuals list and voice direction.
4. The build, in 16:9 and 9:16, each with and without voiceover.
5. At least three critique rounds before the final 60 fps render.
