# Phase 1: Concept

## The idea in one line

Show the connect-your-domain moment from the end user's side. Today it is a jagged path that leaves the app and ends in a DNS panel. With CustomDomain™ it is one straight line: type the domain, sign in to the provider inside the widget, approve, done, and kept that way.

## Who is watching

Product and engineering leads at platforms that offer custom domains (site builders, e-commerce, creator tools, agencies, AI app builders). They know the feature exists; they have not watched their own customers struggle through it. The film makes them watch.

## What they should leave knowing

1. Domains are bought everywhere: over a hundred places, and most people use more than one. Your app cannot know where a given customer's domain lives.
2. So the usual flow hands the customer a list of DNS records and a job: remember the provider, leave the app, sign in elsewhere, find the DNS settings, copy records by hand, wait, hope. Every step is a place to drop off.
3. CustomDomain™ detects where the domain is managed and lets the customer sign in to that provider inside the embeddable widget. Approving once authorizes CustomDomain™ to write the records, verify them, issue HTTPS, and keep watching.
4. The difference, in one picture: a jagged path versus a straight line.

## The visual metaphor

A single ink line is the customer's journey.

- **The usual way:** the line zigzags across the page from station to station (seven stations), doubles back, and ends in a scribble.
- **With CustomDomain™:** the same line pulls straight. Three stations, then "live", then a small loop that keeps running (monitoring).

Everything is drawn in black line on paper, in a light hand-drawn style, with text that writes or rises on. It is calm and informative, with no flashy effects. That is also the brand's own register: the brand page describes the mark as "drawn in ink and paper only".

## Accuracy guardrails (checked against docs.customdomain.ai)

- One-click sign-in (OAuth) exists for a subset of providers (Cloudflare among them), so the demo domain is on Cloudflare and the narration says "sign in to that provider", never "every provider".
- "Over a hundred places to buy a domain": CustomDomain™ catalogues 180+ DNS hosts, registrars and platforms, including all of the 100 largest registrars.
- "Most use more than one registrar": the premise as given in the brief.
- Monitoring: hourly checks, drift fixed and flagged, certificates renewed ahead of expiry (homepage copy).
