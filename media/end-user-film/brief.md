# Brief: CustomDomain™, the end user's path (line-drawn explainer)

- **Goal:** put the spotlight on the end user. In an app, they click "Connect domain"; the usual flow is jagged and sends them to update DNS records by hand at their registrar. With CustomDomain™ the widget detects where the domain is managed and lets them sign in to that provider inside the app; approving once authorizes CustomDomain™ to write, verify and secure the records, and keep them that way.
- **Context to land:** domains can be bought from over 100 websites, and most people use more than one registrar.
- **Style (asked for):** informative, visually simple, not extravagant. Text animation, line drawing, black-line illustration. Here: ink on paper, hand-drawn (rough.js), drawn on along each line, in the brand's faces (Instrument Serif, Inter, Geist Mono); green only for "done".
- **Length:** over one minute; 1:44 as timed to the voice.
- **Deliverables:** 16:9 and 9:16, each with and without voiceover (same picture; the captions stand alone without the voice).
- **Voice:** Kokoro af_heart (local open model), warm and unhurried, 0.85× speed.
- **Accuracy:** one-click sign-in exists for some providers (Cloudflare is the demo); never "every provider".
