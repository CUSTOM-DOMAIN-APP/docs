# Brief: CustomDomain™ problem explainer

- **Product:** CustomDomain™ (https://customdomain.ai). Lets a platform's users connect a domain they already own in one click: detects the DNS provider, writes the records over provider sign-in, verifies them, issues HTTPS.
- **Goal:** a 30 to 45 s explainer that is *truly about the problem*: DNS onboarding dies at the customer, because the customer is the integration layer ("human middleware") between your app and their DNS host.
- **Duration:** 42 s (84 beats at 120 bpm). **Formats:** 16:9 and 9:16.
- **Brand:** customdomain.ai/brand: paper #fafaf9, ink #1c1917, night #0c0a09, warm stone greys; green #16a34a marks a finished step and nothing else; errors are inverted, never red. Instrument Serif (display, italic for emphasis), Inter (UI), Geist Mono (records). The tree mark from tree-mark.svg.
- **Music:** synthesized in code. **Voiceover:** none: the film is carried by kinetic type, like the brand's own 29 s "no sound" intro film, and works muted in a feed.
- **Reference grammar:** the launch films catalogued on whatships.com (kinetic serif type + one morphing UI container + hard cuts on bar lines); the Motion Reel Kit's rules (springs, seek(t), beat grid). Content is original.
- **Must show:** the real connect-widget flow (rebuilt from the live widget), the real tree mark and wordmark, the brand's own claims (150+ providers, 40+ automatic, 0 DNS panels).
- **Must avoid:** red errors, a second accent, invented claims, other brands' marks except the provider logos the widget itself ships.
