# Phase 2: Research, references and tools

## Screen-life, the grammar
- **What it is:** screen-life (also called the computer screen film) tells a story entirely through screens. Bekmambetov's *Unfriended* (2014) popularised it. *Searching* (2018) is the reference for emotion carried by interface: a father searching his missing daughter's laptop, "seen only through a monitor" ([Wikipedia: Screenlife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screenlife), [Gulf News on Bekmambetov](https://gulfnews.com/tabloid/film/timur-bekmambetov-seeks-a-new-cinematic-language-in-searching-1.2279224)).
- **Bekmambetov's rules** ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Screenlife)):
  - one screen that never leaves the frame
  - camerawork that behaves like a device camera
  - real time with no visible cuts
  - sound only from the computer
- **Taken from these:** one screen, real time, and sound mostly from the device (notifications, keys, sends). The voice is the character thinking.
- **Common tools** ([Filmdaft](https://filmdaft.com/what-are-screenlife-movies-definition-and-examples/)): chat threads typed live, notifications, searches, windows and desktop clutter. The emotion lives in what the character types and deletes, and in what they don't send.
- **Taken from these:** Sam types a careful reply, hesitates and rewrites it. The queue counter ticks up while they type.

## Pace and length
- **About 60 to 65 seconds,** with a slow, quiet narration at about 120 to 130 words a minute. The night is unhurried and tired. The morning moves a little faster.
- **A clear before and after:** dark mode at 2:13 a.m., light mode a month later.

## Voice spec
- **Voice:** `af_nicole`, chosen over five other voices for its close, unforced softness. It sounds like someone thinking, not presenting.
- **Speed:** 1.1×. Its natural pace is very slow, and this lands it at about 120 to 130 words a minute.
- **Cadence:**
  - long pauses at night
  - a beat of silence before "So we shipped Custom Domain."
  - the last line warm and dry, with a hint of a smile
