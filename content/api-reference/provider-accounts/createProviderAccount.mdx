      - content: >-
          Keeps a DNS provider or registrar key for the workspace, encrypted
          (GRANT_ENCRYPTION_KEY; `503` without it). The key is checked first by
          listing the provider's domains, or, for a provider that cannot list
          them, by reading one of the selected domains or `check_domain`; with
          neither, `400` `check_domain_required`, and a key is never kept
          unchecked. `access` is `continuous` (kept until revoked, used only to
          put back records it wrote that disappear where the deployment has
          automatic repair on, remove records when a domain is removed, and
          re-apply the records it wrote on request) or `one_time` (usable for an
          hour, and deleted as soon as the chosen domains are connected or the
          hour passes). `scope` is `all` of the account's domains or `selected`
          ones. The key is never returned.


          Anyone whose role operates applications may connect a key, read the
          accounts and revoke one. Changing an account's access, scope or
          domains, connecting domains with it and replacing its key take an
          admin, an owner or the member who connected it (`can_manage`).
