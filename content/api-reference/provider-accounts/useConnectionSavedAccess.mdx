          Writes the connection's records with one of the kept keys `GET /connections/{id}/saved-access` answers for it (`account_id`), and links the key to the domain. A key offered with `ready: false` takes the domain's zone into its scope once the write worked. An account the resolver does not answer for this domain and caller is `409` `saved_access_not_usable`. A write that fails leaves the domain and the key as they were: `502` `saved_access_write_failed`.
