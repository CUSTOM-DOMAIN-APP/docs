    contents:
      - content: >-
          A new key for an account, in one of two ways (`method`). Both take an admin,
          an owner or the member who connected the account (`403` otherwise), and the
          key is recorded in the audit log (`provider_account.key_replaced`).
      - content: >-
          `api_key` (the default), for a key a person pasted: for an account the
          provider stopped accepting, or a one time account whose key was deleted.
          Everything is checked before anything is kept: the access mode, then the key
          (listing the provider's domains, or reading `check_domain`, a selected
          domain or a domain the account connected, for a provider that cannot list
          them), and for a `selected` account that the key reaches every selected
          domain. `params` sent with the key replace the kept ones. A key made at a
          secure sign-in answers `409`: it is renewed by signing in again, not by
          pasting a key. `continuous` access (asked, or the account's own) for a
          credential that is used once and never kept answers `422`
          `use_once_credential`; send `access` `one_time`.
      - content: >-
          `secure_sign_in`, for a key made at a secure sign-in a member started in the
          console: starts the same account sign in as `POST
          /provider-accounts:sign-in`, for the account's own provider and application,
          and answers `201` with `session_id` and `popup_url`. The new key swaps into
          this same account when the person keeps it (`POST
          /browser-sessions/{sid}:keep` with an empty body), so the account keeps its
          id, its domains, its scope and the domains linked to it; only its key
          changes, it is active again with its daily check started afresh, and the key
          it replaces is named for deletion at the provider (`replaced_key`). It is
          the console's own call (an API key on the signed visitor forward, `403`
          `browser_account_unavailable` otherwise) and takes the sign in's body
          (`return_origin`, `consent_version`, `locale`, `viewport_hint`) and no key.
          A key a person pasted is renewed by pasting (`409`), and a key a customer's
          own secure sign-in made is renewed by that customer, never from the console
          (`403`). The keep refuses a key of another provider account (`422`
          `different_account`): where the provider names the account, the two must be
          the same; where it does not (IONOS), the new key must list every domain the
          account holds. An account that holds no domain and has no account number
          gives nothing to compare, so its renewal is refused (`422`
          `account_not_verifiable`): remove the account and add it again with a new
          sign in. The refused key stays pending and is named for deletion when the
          sign in is cancelled.
