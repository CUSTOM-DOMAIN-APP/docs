          The popup reports one step of its window, for the sign in funnel: the
          sheet shown, a code or push prompt, a social sign in card, the typing
          overlay, the hand back for a password, the manual steps taken, the
          window closed. The control plane records it with the states it writes
          itself and counts it per provider. It changes nothing about the
          session and is not a heartbeat. `detail` is one short word (an `sms`,
          a `google`), never anything the customer typed, a page, an address or
          a URL: the closed alphabet refuses a space, a slash, a question mark,
          an equals sign and an at sign. A session that has ended still takes a
          step (the window closes after the end) while it answers its last view.
          At most 30 steps a minute per session. A `navigator.sendBeacon` cannot
          set headers, so, as for `:cancel`, the token may come in the body as
          `token` with a content type of `text/plain` or JSON.
