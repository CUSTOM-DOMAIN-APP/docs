      - content: >-
          The customer signed in to the provider with an account that does not
          hold the domain (the view says `wrongAccount`), and tries another one.
          Allowed only then. The control plane signs the cloud browser out of
          the provider in the background (the provider's own sign out page,
          then the browser forgets the provider's cookies and storage, never a
          social sign in's) and opens the provider's sign in again; meanwhile
          the view says `notice` `switching_account` and takes no input. The
          sign in window starts again; the hard cap does not move. At most
          three accounts are tried: the third that does not hold the domain
          ends the session (`failed`, `error.code` `browser_wrong_account`),
          and so does a sign out that fails. No body.
