      - content: >-
          On a session whose vendor never shows its page (`no_live_page`:
          Kernel), the customer pressed our form's "Continue with Google" or
          "Continue with Apple". The control plane opens the vendor's managed
          sign in of a profile of this session's own to the identity provider's
          site, and the view's `idp` carries the connection and its single use
          `handoff_code`: the popup renders the vendor's form, which talks to
          the vendor from the customer's browser (no password, code or approval
          passes through CustomDomain™). On success the remote browser is
          released and made again on the signed in profile, back on the
          provider's sign in page (`idp.state` `finishing`), and the popup
          forwards the customer's press of the provider's own button over the
          relay; the identity provider asks the customer to confirm the account
          (the one page the popup frames on such a session), and the sign in
          notice carries on as usual. Allowed only in `awaiting_sign_in`, with
          no step running (a failed one may be tried again), for a kind the
          provider's form offers.
