      - content: >-
          Available on every plan (it is how a Connect-only plan gets domains
          that serve). `cname_target` must be a public hostname that is not the
          platform edge; send `""` to go back to the edge. `apex_ipv4` (up to 4
          public IPv4 addresses) is what root domains get on DNS providers with
          no ALIAS-style record; without it such roots cannot be pointed at a
          hostname and `POST /domains:check` reports `apex_strategy:
          unsupported`.


          Applies to new connections and to domains still waiting for DNS
          (pending or failed, with no records supplied or written for them).
          Connected domains keep the routing they connected with (their
          `routing` field); move one with `POST /v1/connections/{id}:retarget`.
          `existing_domains` in the response counts both.


          Refused (400) when `cname_target` resolves to the platform edge (a
          CNAME to it, or one of its addresses), or when `apex_ipv4` lists an
          edge address: such domains would verify but never be served. A
          `cname_target` that does not resolve yet, or a target with no
          `apex_ipv4`, is accepted with a `warnings` entry. Every change is
          recorded in the audit log (`app.routing_set`, `app.routing_cleared`).
