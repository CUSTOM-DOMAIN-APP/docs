          `mode: edge` (the default) points customer domains at the platform
          edge, which proxies their traffic (Power). `mode: custom` points them
          at your own infrastructure: subdomains get a CNAME to `cname_target`;
          a root domain gets an ALIAS, ANAME or flattened CNAME to it on
          providers that have one, and A records to `apex_ipv4` elsewhere.
          `edge` echoes what edge mode points at, so a console can show both
          options.
