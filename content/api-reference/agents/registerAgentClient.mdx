          Dynamic Client Registration (RFC 7591). An AI assistant registers
          itself as a public client (PKCE, no secret) and can do nothing until a
          person approves a grant for it in the console. Registration is open on
          purpose, so it is bounded: a per-IP hourly budget on top of the global
          limit, and a client that nobody ever approves into a workspace is
          deleted after a while. Other client metadata (`client_uri`,
          `logo_uri`, `scope`, `contacts`, ...) is accepted and ignored. A
          `client_name` that claims a well known brand (Claude, ChatGPT, ...) is
          refused unless every redirect host belongs to that brand or is a
          loopback address. Errors use the RFC 7591 envelope.
