          Clears the forward and the root path access, drops the origin and tears
          down the certificate. Clearing a forward is checked like any redirect
          change: on a domain whose `www` redirect is on, the visit that went to
          the forward now goes to the `www` name, and when that would come back
          (`www` forwards to the root) the call is refused with `422`
          `redirect_loop` and nothing is changed, the origin included. The `www`
          redirect itself is not cleared here (`PATCH /connections/{id}` turns it
          off).
