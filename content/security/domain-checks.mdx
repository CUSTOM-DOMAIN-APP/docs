CustomDomainBot is how CustomDomain™ checks whether a domain that someone entered
responds on the public internet. If you found `CustomDomainBot/1.0` in your server
logs or your DNS query logs, this page says what it did and what we keep.

## Why it visits

When a person enters your domain in CustomDomain™ (to search for it, check it, price
it, connect it or buy it), or signs up with an address at your company's domain, we
look at the domain's public DNS records and make one ordinary request for its home
page. We do it to help connect the domain, and to tell a live domain from a parked,
redirecting or dead one. We have no interest in what your site says.

## What it requests

- **Public DNS.** The domain's addresses, nameservers, mail and certificate
  authorization records, and its SPF and DMARC records. We read no other TXT record.
  The questions go to public resolvers, never to servers your DNS named.
- **One web request.** `GET /` over HTTPS, or over HTTP when HTTPS cannot connect,
  shake hands or answer in time. It follows up to five redirects and then stops. It
  never asks for another path, never submits a form, never sends cookies or
  credentials and never sends a referrer.
- **Only ordinary ports.** It connects to ports 80 and 443 on public addresses, and
  refuses anything private, internal or reserved, including on a redirect.
- **A plain introduction.** `User-Agent: CustomDomainBot/1.0 (+https://docs.customdomain.ai/docs/security/domain-checks)`.
- **Rarely.** At most once a day for a domain, however many people entered it. A
  domain that does not answer is tried again after a longer and longer wait, up to a
  day.

## What we keep

The DNS records we read, whether the domain answered and with what status, where it
redirects (without query strings), the type and size of the page, whether its
certificate was valid, and the page title. We read at most the first 64 KiB of an
HTML page and keep nothing from it but the title. We keep no cookies, no other
headers and no page content, and the result is a fact about the domain, not about any
person or account.

## Ask us to stop

Write to [privacy@customdomain.ai](mailto:privacy@customdomain.ai) with the domain.
We stop checking it and delete what we hold on it. You can also block CustomDomainBot
at your server or firewall: it identifies itself in the `User-Agent` header, and
nothing breaks if it is refused.

The [Privacy Policy](https://app.customdomain.ai/privacy) describes domain checks in
section 3.3.
