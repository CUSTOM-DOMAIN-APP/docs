**Overview** in the console shows a go-live checklist until your first real
domain is connected. Each row says what to do and turns done when it is done.

## The four stages [#the-four-stages]

1. **Try the widget.** Connect a made up name in the Sandbox. See [Sandbox](/docs/guides/test-mode).
2. **Mint tokens.** Have your server ask for a widget token. See [Widget tokens](/docs/authentication/widget-tokens).
3. **Set hosting.** Say where your customers' domains point. See [Where domains point](/docs/connect-flow/routing#through-the-edge).
4. **Connect a user domain.** Connect the first real domain. See [Create a connection](/docs/getting-started/quickstart#create-a-connection).

## The go-live list [#the-go-live-list]

After sign up, the list on **Overview** has five rows, in this order.

| Row | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| **Add the widget** | Put the widget on your page and check it works. See [Widget](/docs/getting-started/widget). |
| **Go live** | Pick a plan. See [Plans and quotas](/docs/billing/plans-and-quotas#checkout--portal). |
| **Choose hosting** | Pick **Hosted by CustomDomain™** or **Your servers**. See [Hosting modes](/docs/guides/hosting-modes). |
| **Brand the widget** | Save a look for your application. See [Designing the widget](/docs/widget-sdk/designing-the-widget). |
| **First real domain** | Connect a customer's domain. See [Create a connection](/docs/getting-started/quickstart#create-a-connection). |

You can stay in the Sandbox until you are ready. Real customer domains need a plan.

**Dismiss** hides the list for you. **Help**, then **Show the setup list**, brings it back.
