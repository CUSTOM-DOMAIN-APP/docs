These figures were read from `GET https://api.customdomain.ai/v1/plans` on
2026-10-31. If this page and that endpoint disagree, the endpoint is right.

| Plan | Price | Domains a year | Domains a month | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $10 a month | 10 | 1 | Connect |
| Startup | $149 a month | 600 | 50 | Connect |
| Growth | $649 a month | 600 | 50 | Connect, serving through the edge (Power), certificates (Secure) |
| Premium | No public price (sales) | 12,000 | 1,000 | Growth's, plus Monitor (listed, not enforced) |
| Enterprise | Custom (sales) | 12,000 or more | 1,000 | Premium's, plus white-label |

- **A new workspace's first ten domains are free**, with no card. A card is asked
  for when you create an API key, create an application or connect an eleventh
  domain, and that starts a **14 day free trial**. Canceling before it ends costs
  nothing.
- **Starter, Startup and Growth** are bought in the console through Stripe
  Checkout. Premium and Enterprise start with a demo.
- **The allowance is metered by month**: the yearly number divided by 12, rounded
  up. No plan above Starter is ever refused a connection at its allowance; a
  connection past it is recorded and shown, not blocked.
- **Connected domains keep working** whatever the billing state: traffic,
  certificate renewals and monitoring carry on.
- **Not on any plan:** SSO, SCIM and uptime monitoring.
