Connecting a domain points its web traffic at a product. The email records (`MX`,
and `TXT` or `CNAME` records for SPF, DKIM and DMARC) can be added the same way, in
the same connection.

## What CustomDomain™ does

- **Merges SPF instead of adding a second record.** A domain must have exactly one
  `v=spf1` record. An `SPFM` record adds your mechanisms to the record already
  published, deduplicated, with one terminal `all` and the existing policy kept, so
  a merge never loosens it. A merge that would pass 10 DNS lookups or 255 characters
  is refused (`spf_merge_failed`).
- **Fills the standard records for three providers.** Google Workspace, Microsoft
  365 (with your tenant's MX host) and Zoho. For Zoho's EU data center the MX host
  is `mx.zoho.eu` and the SPF include `spf.zoho.eu`.
- **Takes settings, never raw records,** on the automatic rails and the MCP tool
  `add-email`: a mail host, an SPF include, a DKIM selector and target, a DMARC
  report address. Each value is checked before anything is written.
- **Warns before writing.** The pre-flight (`POST /v1/domains:check`) reports
  conflicting records it sees in public DNS, SPF clashes included.

## What it does not do

- **DKIM keys come from your mail provider.** They are unique per domain and made in
  the provider's admin console, so they cannot be derived. Add the selector record
  the provider gives you.
- **DMARC policy is yours to tighten.** Start at `p=none`, confirm that legitimate
  mail passes DKIM and SPF, then move to `quarantine` and `reject`.
