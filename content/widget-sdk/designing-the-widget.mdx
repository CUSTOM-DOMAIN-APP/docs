**Widget** then **Design** is where the widget gets your look. The preview is the
widget your customers get, running on example data. Nothing you do there changes
a real domain.

## What you can set

- **Brand:** your name and logo.
- **Colors:** the color for buttons and links.
- **Shape and type:** corner roundness and width.
- **Behavior:** light, dark or match the customer's device.

Switch the preview between computer and phone, light and dark, and step through
the screens a customer sees, from entering a domain to connected.

## Each application has its own look

Design edits the application chosen in the sidebar. **Save** gives that
application its own look. **Save as the workspace default** sets the look for
every application that has none of its own.

## When your customers see it

A saved look reaches your customers the next time the widget opens. Changes you
have not saved show only in the preview. Owners and admins can save. Everyone
else sees the design but cannot change it.

For the widget's code options, see [Installing and embedding the widget](/docs/widget-sdk/installation-and-embed).
