An origin is the address of a website: `https://app.yourcompany.com`. An
application keeps a list of the sites that may open the widget for your
customers.

## Add an origin

Add one for each site where your product opens the widget, including staging.

- Start with `https://`.
- Give the host, and a port only if it is not the usual one.
- Leave out any path, and do not use a wildcard.
- You can list up to 20.

`https://app.yourcompany.com` is valid. `https://yourcompany.com/app` and
`https://*.yourcompany.com` are not.

## If the check does not pass

The setup check on [Widget](/docs/getting-started/widget#check-it-works) may wait
because the site where you opened the widget is not on the list. Add its address
and try again.
