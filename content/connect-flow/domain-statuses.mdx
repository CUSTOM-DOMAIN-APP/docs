**Domains** in the console shows one status for each domain. The three filters
use the same words.

| Status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Live** | The domain is connected and your customer's visitors reach it. | Nothing. |
| **In progress** | The domain is still being set up, for example while it waits for DNS records. | Wait. We keep checking. |
| **Needs attention** | Somebody should look at it. | Open the domain. The row says why. |

## Needs attention [#needs-attention]

A domain needs attention when its setup failed or stopped, its records were
changed at the provider, or it is still being set up 48 hours after it was
added.

A live domain needs attention too when its certificate is failing or about to
expire, a change to its records is waiting for you, its provider stopped
accepting our access, or it has nothing to serve yet.

A live domain that needs attention shows under both **Live** and **Needs
attention**.

Fix the cause shown on the row. The status clears by itself once it is fixed.
For the steps, see [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) and
[Go live](/docs/connect-flow/verify-and-go-live).
