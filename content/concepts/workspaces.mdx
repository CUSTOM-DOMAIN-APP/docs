A workspace is your team's space on CustomDomain™. It holds your people,
billing, applications and domains. Open **Settings**, then **General**, to see it.

## The name [#the-name]

The name shows to your team. An owner or an admin can rename it under **General**.
Renaming it does not rename any application.

## The workspace ID [#the-workspace-id]

**General** shows the **Workspace ID** with a copy button. Give it to support
when you write to us, so we find the right workspace.

## The owner [#the-owner]

The person who made the workspace is its owner. A workspace can have more than
one. Owners can do everything an admin can, and can also transfer or delete the
workspace. See [Team and roles](/docs/guides/team-and-roles#transfer-the-workspace).

## Deleting a workspace [#deleting-a-workspace]

Only an owner can delete a workspace. The console does not offer it yet. To
delete yours, write to connect@customdomain.ai from an owner's email address and
name the workspace.
