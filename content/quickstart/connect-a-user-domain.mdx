---
title: "4. Connect a user's domain"
description: "A real domain goes live for one of your users."
metaDescription: "Stage 4 of the CustomDomain™ Quickstart: a real user connects their own domain through the widget and it goes live."
---

Now a real user connects a domain they own, through your product.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the widget for your user

Mint a token as in [stage 2](/docs/quickstart/mint-tokens) and open the widget
for them.

</Step>

<Step>

### Watch it go live

Your user enters their domain and follows the widget. It finds their provider,
adds the records where it can, and shows the rest. See
[verify and go live](/docs/connect-flow/verify-and-go-live).

</Step>

</Steps>

**Done when** a real user's domain shows **Live**.

Then add a [webhook](/docs/webhooks/overview) to hear about each connection, and
[brand the widget](/docs/widget-sdk/reference).
