Four stages take you from a first try to a customer's domain going live. Each one
ends with something you can see.

<Cards>
  <Card title="1. Try the widget" href="/docs/quickstart/try-the-widget" description="A made up domain goes live. No card." />
  <Card title="2. Mint tokens" href="/docs/quickstart/mint-tokens" description="Your server opens the widget for a user." />

  <Card title="4. Connect a user's domain" href="/docs/quickstart/connect-a-user-domain" description="A real domain goes live." />

Prefer a terminal, with cURL or Node, from the first request? The
[terminal walkthrough](/docs/getting-started/quickstart) covers stage 1 that way.

After the fourth stage, add a [webhook](/docs/webhooks/overview) and
[brand the widget](/docs/widget-sdk/reference).
