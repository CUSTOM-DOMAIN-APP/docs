**Analytics** shows how your customers connect their domains. Every tab follows
the date range and the application picker at the top.

<OpenInConsole to="/app/analytics" label="Open Analytics in console" />

## The funnel [#the-funnel]

The **Widget** tab counts what happens each time the widget opens. The steps are
a domain entered, a way to connect offered, records shown, and connected.

**Most common stopping point** names the step where people most often closed the
widget. Start there when fewer customers finish than you expect.

The tab also says whether the widget has been seen in your product. Your own
clicks in **Try it live** do not count.

## Traffic [#traffic]

**Traffic** counts requests to domains that CustomDomain™ hosts for you. A
request is one page, image or file that a visitor's browser asked for.

It shows how many requests there were, what share was served or redirected, and
how much data was sent. Under it, the busiest domains and how many of their
requests were not served. Search engines and scanners count too.

## Providers [#providers]

**Providers** shows where domains stall, by the DNS provider the customer uses.
Each row lists how many domains started, how many connected and the most common
reason the rest are stuck. It also shows how long domains take to go live.

## Secure sign-in [#secure-sign-in]

This tab appears once customers have used [secure sign-in](/docs/guides/secure-sign-in).
It shows how many finished, how long it took and where people stopped.
