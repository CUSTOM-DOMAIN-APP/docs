CustomDomain™ tells you when something happens to your domains, your billing or
your agents, so you do not have to keep a tab open. There are two places a
notification can land, and you choose each one separately:

- **The bell.** The bell at the top right of the console holds your latest
  notifications, with a count of the ones you have not opened. **See all
  notifications** opens the full list. Nothing here asks for a permission.
- **Push.** A notification from your browser, on your phone or computer, even
  when the console is closed. Push is off until you turn it on in a browser, and
  your browser asks you first.

Email is separate from both. [Email](#email), below, lists every email we send,
who gets it and how to stop it. Security and account email is always sent.

| Notification | When | In the bell | Push |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A domain goes live | A domain finishes connecting and is serving your customers. | On | On |
| A domain needs attention | A domain did not finish connecting after a day, or is still waiting for DNS records. | On | On |
| DNS records changed | A record we manage changed or went missing and you have to put it right. | On | On |
| DNS records put back | We restored records that went missing, through access you kept. | On | On |
| Certificate problems | A certificate could not be issued, keeps failing to renew, is about to expire or has expired. | On | On |
| A payment fails, choose a plan reminders, adding domains is paused, billing is back in good standing, your trial is ending | Each rings when its [billing email](#billing-emails) is sent. A few billing emails have no bell: the trial starting, a subscription ending and an ended plan's three emails. | On | On |
| A refund or dispute is under review | A payment is refunded or disputed and a person is looking at it. Nothing changes on its own. | On | On |
| A domain you bought is registered, or the purchase failed | A purchase through your workspace finished or could not be completed. | On | On |
| An agent spent money, or a new agent was connected | An AI agent bought a domain, or someone gave one access to the workspace. | On | On |
| An agent's access ended | A person or the agent revoked the agent's access to the workspace. | On | On |
| An agent hit its spend cap | An agent tried to buy a domain past the monthly cap you set. The purchase is refused and no money moves. | On | On |
| An agent's credential was reused | An old agent credential was used again after it was replaced. The agent's access is revoked. | On | On |
| A DNS provider needs reconnecting | A provider stopped accepting a saved key, so automatic repair is paused. | On | On |
| A DNS provider key ends soon | An [API key a secure sign-in made](/docs/guides/secure-sign-in#reconnect) at a DNS provider ends in 30 or 7 days, so its end user can sign in again first. | On | On |
| Routine agent actions | What an agent does, such as connecting a domain, once an hour. | On | Off |
| A customer connects through your widget | One of your customers connected a domain with the widget. A domain you connected yourself with the widget, from the welcome, is told here too, as that domain being live. | On | Off |
| Someone joins your team | A person you invited accepted. | On | Off |

These go to the **owners and admins** of the workspace. A member or a viewer
does not get them. The last three are quiet by default because they can be
frequent; turn push on for them if you want it. The exception is a workspace for
your own product's customers (you chose **My product's users** at the start): the
first browser you turn push on in also pushes for **A customer connects through
your widget**, because that is the moment you are waiting for. It is an ordinary
choice on your notification page, **Settings → Notifications** (with the new
menu, **Notification settings** in the avatar menu): turn it off there and it
stays off.

If you belong to more than one workspace, the bell holds them all, each
notification starts with its workspace's name, and opening one switches the
console to that workspace.

A domain going live is the one worth leaving the page for. While a domain waits
for DNS, its page offers **Get a ping when it is live**, so you can close the tab
and be told the moment it is connected. It turns push on for that one thing; you
choose the rest under **Settings → Notifications** (with the new menu,
**Notification settings** in the avatar menu). A domain a customer connected with
the widget has no card, because those stay in the bell.

The welcome asks the same thing on its last screen, while a domain you just added
waits for its DNS records: **Get a ping when acme.com is live**, or "when your
domains go live" after you pasted a list, with **Notify me** and **Not now**.
**Notify me** is the click that makes your browser ask, and it turns push on for a
domain going live and for nothing else. Turning push on there, or blocking
notifications at your browser's prompt, ends the offer in that browser, so the one
on **Overview** does not ask again. **Not now** counts like **Not now** on
**Overview** (below), so that offer comes back a few days later. Signing out of a
browser that had push on takes the answer back, so you are offered it again the next
time you sign in there. Where the browser cannot do push or has blocked it, the
screen says nothing about it.

## Turn push on

After you sign up, **Overview** also offers **Get notified in this browser**, with
**Turn on** and **Not now**. It shows only where the browser can do push, and only
after the other one-time offers on **Overview** (such as adding Google or GitHub)
have been answered. **Turn on** is the click that makes your browser ask.

**Not now** brings the offer back on a later visit to **Overview**: at least 3 days
after the first **Not now**, at least 7 days after the second, and not by itself
after the third. Once you have said **Not now** in a browser (here or on the
welcome), the offer also has **Don't ask again**. Turning push on, blocking
notifications at your browser's prompt, or **Don't ask again** ends the offer in
that browser. A site cannot ask a browser that blocked it again, so the offer then
links **How to allow them** to **Settings → Notifications** (with the new menu,
**Notification settings** in the avatar menu). You can also turn push on
yourself:

1. Open **Settings → Notifications** (with the new menu,
   **Notification settings** in the avatar menu).
2. Press **Enable notifications on this device**. Your browser asks whether to
   allow notifications; choose Allow. It only asks when you press the button.
3. Press **Send a test notification** to see one arrive.

Push is per device. Do the same in each browser you want it in; the list under
**Devices with notifications on** shows every one, and **Remove** takes one off.

**On an iPhone or iPad**, notifications work once CustomDomain™ is added to your
Home Screen: in Safari tap Share, choose Add to Home Screen, then open
CustomDomain™ from your Home Screen and turn push on there. Safari itself
cannot receive web push.

**If your browser blocks notifications for the site**, we cannot turn them on
from the page. Open the browser's site settings (the icon next to the address),
set Notifications to Allow, then come back and check again.

## Choose what you are told

Open **Settings → Notifications** (with the new menu, **Notification settings**
in the avatar menu). Under **What to tell you** every kind has two switches,
**In app** and **Push**. A switch you never touched follows the default above.
Changes apply at once and are yours alone: they do not change what a teammate
gets.

## Turn them off

- **One kind, one place:** switch it off under **What to tell you**.
- **Push on this device:** press **Turn off on this device**, or remove it from
  the device list.
- **Push on every device:** remove each device, or switch **Push** off for every
  kind.
- **Everything but the bell:** switch **Push** off for every kind.
- **Signing out** takes this browser off the device list, so a shared computer
  stops getting your notifications.

You can also withdraw the permission in your browser's site settings at any
time. A notification never needs you to keep the console open.

## Email

Every email CustomDomain™ sends is listed below: what it says and when it goes,
who gets it, whether it is always sent or optional, and how to stop it. The
console shows the same list, read only and open to every role, so a member sees
what an owner sees. Nothing on it switches an email on or off.

- Open **Settings → Notifications**. Your own switches are at the top: push on
  this device, and the bell and push choice for each kind of alert, which the
  steps above describe. Under them, **What we send** has one line per kind of
  email and a closed row, **Every email, who gets it and how to stop it**, which
  opens the full list.
- The same switches are on **Notification settings**, in the avatar menu.

There is one menu now, so the separate **Emails we send** page of the old Classic
menu is gone. [Navigation](/docs/getting-started/console#navigation) has the
details.

Beside each email the console also shows whether the bell and push ring at the
same moment, and what they default to. They go to every owner and admin, so when
an email goes to one person, such as the person who gave an agent access, the
page adds "for owners and admins". You choose them under **What to tell you**,
above, not here.

### How to stop an email

The last column of each table says how. There are three cases:

- **Always sent.** Security and account email cannot be turned off. Its row says
  what to do instead: revoke an agent, change a person's role on the **Team**
  page (**Team & roles** in the new menu), fix the domain, or ignore the email.
- **Optional: tips and product updates, domain alerts, the weekly digest.** Each
  has one switch in your **Email preferences**. Open it from the **Email
  preferences** link in the footer of one of those emails (no sign-in needed), or
  from the link at the top of the console's list. **Unsubscribe from all optional
  email** turns all three off at once.
- **Optional: set for each application.** Email to your customers is stopped by
  the application's own setting, not by anyone's preferences: choose **Don't
  send** under **Who sends DNS update emails**, in **End user email** on the
  application's settings. See
  [Who sends it](/docs/connect-flow/end-user-notices#who-sends-it).

move email, and [End user notices](/docs/connect-flow/end-user-notices)
describes the two emails to a customer.

| Email | Who gets it | Sent | How to stop it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A domain needs attention.** A domain has waited a day for its DNS records and they are still missing. Not sent once the domain is a week old. | The workspace owner | Optional: domain alerts | Turn off domain alerts in your email preferences. |
| **A domain's setup failed.** A domain's setup failed and stayed failed for two hours. Not sent once the failure is a week old. | The workspace owner | Optional: domain alerts | Turn off domain alerts in your email preferences. |
| **Your first domain is live.** The first time a domain goes live, within a week of it. | The workspace owner | Optional: tips and product updates | Turn off tips and product updates in your email preferences. |
| **Certificate problems.** A certificate could not be issued, keeps failing to renew, is about to expire or has expired. | Owners and admins | Always sent | Cannot be turned off. Fix the domain and the alerts stop. To stop it for one person, change their role to member on the **Team** page. |
| **DNS records changed.** A record CustomDomain™ manages changed or went missing and you have to put it right. It is also sent when a domain's nameservers were changed at the registrar after a [nameserver move](/docs/guides/nameserver-migration). | Owners and admins | Always sent | Cannot be turned off. Fix the records and the alerts stop. To stop it for one person, change their role to member on the **Team** page. |
| **A domain moved to other routing.** A domain was moved to other routing and its DNS records changed. | Owners and admins | Always sent | Cannot be turned off. It is sent once for each move. To stop it for one person, change their role to member on the **Team** page. |
| **DNS records sent to someone.** Someone asked us to email a domain's DNS records, from the widget or the console. | The person it is for | Always sent | Cannot be turned off. It is sent only when someone asks, to the address they typed. Ignore it and nothing changes. |
| **The weekly digest.** Once a week, early in the week, when the week had widget activity, a new domain or something that needs attention. | The workspace owner | Optional: the weekly digest | Turn off the weekly digest in your email preferences. |
| **New DNS records for a customer.** A domain was moved and its records have to change, sent to the customer who connected it. | The customer who connected the domain | Optional: set for each application | Choose **Don't send** under **Who sends DNS update emails** in the application's settings. It stops the reconnect email too. Or leave the customer's address off: it goes only to the address you gave us. |
| **Reconnect notice for a customer.** The key we made at a customer's DNS provider is about to end or has stopped working. | The customer who connected the domain | Optional: set for each application | Choose **Don't send** under **Who sends DNS update emails** in the application's settings. It stops the new DNS records email too. Or leave the customer's address off: it goes only to the address you gave us. |

### Agent emails

The email goes to the person who gave the agent access. Its bell and push go to
every owner and admin. [Owner emails](/docs/agents/managing-access#owner-emails)
describes them.

| Email | Who gets it | Sent | How to stop it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A new agent was connected.** Someone gave an AI agent access to the workspace. It lists what the agent can do. | The person who gave the agent access | Always sent | Cannot be turned off. It is sent once, when access is given. |
| **Routine agent actions.** An agent connected, disconnected or re-applied a domain, used a saved key, or moved a domain's nameservers. | The person who gave the agent access | Always sent | Cannot be turned off. Revoke the agent's access under **Agents** and these stop. |
| **An agent hit its spend cap.** A purchase was blocked because it would go over the agent's monthly spend cap. No money moved. | The person who gave the agent access | Always sent | Cannot be turned off. Revoke the agent's access under **Agents** and these stop. |
| **An agent spent money.** An AI agent bought a domain and was charged. | The person who gave the agent access | Always sent | Cannot be turned off. Revoke the agent's access under **Agents** and these stop. |
| **An agent's access ended.** An agent's access to the workspace was revoked, by a person or by the agent. | The person who gave the agent access | Always sent | Cannot be turned off. It is sent once, when access ends. |
| **An agent's credential was reused.** An old agent credential was used again after it was replaced. The agent's access is revoked. | The person who gave the agent access | Always sent | Cannot be turned off. It is sent once, when the agent is cut off. |

## What we keep

To send push we store your browser's push endpoint and keys, the name of the
browser (such as Chrome on macOS) so the device list can tell your devices
apart, and when each was added and last used, linked to your account and
workspace. Your browser's user agent is read to make that name and is not
kept. **Remove** deletes them. The bell's notifications are kept for about four
months. See the [privacy policy](https://app.customdomain.ai/privacy) for the
rest.
