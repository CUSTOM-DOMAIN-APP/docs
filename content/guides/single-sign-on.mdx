Single sign-on lets your team sign in to the console with your company's
identity provider, instead of a separate password.

## What it needs [#what-it-needs]

- The **Enterprise** plan. On a lower plan, **Single sign-on** under **Settings**, then **Security**, shows the plan it needs. Compare plans on [Plans and quotas](/docs/billing/plans-and-quotas).
- An owner or an admin.
- A recent sign in, before you change anything.

If the page reads **Not available yet**, it is not switched on for your workspace.
Write to connect@customdomain.ai.

## Set it up [#set-it-up]

1. Open **Settings**, then **Security**, then **Single sign-on**.
2. Add your identity provider and the domain your team signs in with.
3. Prove you control that domain.
4. Turn it on. Choose **Require** only after you have tested it yourself.

Keep one owner who can still sign in with a password, so you are never locked out.

For passwords and passkeys, see [Account security](/docs/guides/account-security).
