Every application serves its customers' domains one of two ways. You pick one
for the application. You can change it later.

<OpenInConsole to="/app/routing" label="Open Routing in console" />

| | Hosted by CustomDomain™ | Your servers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You give us | The address of your app | A hostname, and addresses for bare domains |
| Plans | Edge and up | Every plan |

## Hosted by CustomDomain™ [#hosted-by-customdomain]

We serve your customers' domains and send their visitors to your app.

1. Open **Routing** in the console, then choose **Hosted by CustomDomain™**.
2. Enter **Your app's address**, for example `https://app.yourcompany.com`.
3. Save.

Your customers then add the records the widget shows them, or approve a
one-click setup. You do nothing for each domain.

## Your servers [#your-servers]

Your own servers or load balancer serve the domains, and your customers point
their domains at you.

1. Open **Routing** in the console, then choose **Your servers**.
2. Enter a **Hostname**, for example `domains.yourcompany.com`. Create it in your DNS before your first customer connects.
3. If you want bare domains such as `acme.com` to work for every customer, add up to four **Addresses for the bare domain**. Without them, a bare domain works only where the customer's DNS host offers an ALIAS-style record. See [Root domains](/docs/dns/root-domains).
4. Save.

You issue the certificates for these domains yourself.

## What it needs [#what-it-needs]

- **Hosted by CustomDomain™** is part of the Edge plan and up. On a lower plan the card shows **Edge** and the button reads **Upgrade**. Compare plans on [Plans and quotas](/docs/billing/plans-and-quotas).
- **Your servers** is on every plan.
- On a plan below Edge, the page opens with **Your servers** picked. Until you save a hostname, or upgrade and set your app's address, your customers' domains will not load yet.

## One domain different [#one-domain-different]

Changing the choice for an application does not change domains that are already
connected. Domains still waiting for DNS take the new records. Connected domains
keep working where they are until you move them.

To move one connected domain, open it in the console and choose **Move this
domain**. Your customer then replaces their old records with the new ones. To undo it, use the undo option on the same page.

See [Where domains point](/docs/connect-flow/routing) for the API calls behind
both choices.
