Everything here is in the console under **Settings > Security**.

## Sign-in methods

The ways you can sign in to your account: email and password, a passkey, or Google
or GitHub. Keep at least two, so losing one never locks you out. You can connect
or disconnect Google or GitHub here.

## Passkeys

A passkey signs you in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN instead of a
password. Add one on each device you use, and name it so you can tell them
apart. Removing a passkey stops it from signing you in.

## Password

Change the password you sign in with. You need your current password. You can
also sign out of your other devices at the same time. If you signed up with
Google or GitHub and have no password, use **Email me a link** under sign-in
methods to set one.

## Where you're signed in

The devices where you are signed in, with when each was last active. Sign out
any you don't recognize, or sign out everywhere else at once.

## Confirm it's you

Some changes, like revealing or making an API key or changing a member's role,
may ask you to confirm it's you first if you signed in a while ago. Sign in again in
the box that opens, then the change goes ahead.

## Security emails

We email you when your password changes, and when a way to sign in is added or
removed. If you did not make the change, reset your password and contact
support@customdomain.ai.

## Recent security activity

Your own recent sign-in changes, newest first, so you can check what changed and
when.
