The audit log lists the changes made in your workspace: what changed, who made
the change and when. A change made by an agent shows the agent.

## Read it [#read-it]

Open **Settings**, then **Audit**, to see **Who changed what**. Open a row for its detail.

## Who can read it [#who-can-read-it]

Everyone in the workspace can read it, including viewers.

## What it needs [#what-it-needs]

The audit log is part of the **Scale** and **Enterprise** plans. Compare plans on
[Plans and quotas](/docs/billing/plans-and-quotas).

For the same list through the API, see [Audit log](/docs/api-reference/enterprise/getAudit).
